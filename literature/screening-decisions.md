## What is counted here, and what is not

PRISMA item 16b is the list of full-text exclusions with a specific
reason for each, and it is below in full. What is NOT below is a
reconciling flow diagram from records identified to records included.

The reason is that the number at the top of such a diagram does not
exist for this review. The search ran as 154 API queries
across four concept blocks with heavily overlapping result sets, and no
deduplicated master list of retrieved records was retained. Summing the
per-query yields in `sources/search_record.md` would double-count every
record returned by more than one query, and a diagram whose top box is
a sum of overlapping sets reconciles arithmetically while describing
nothing. So the counts that are real are given, and the one that is
missing is named rather than estimated.

## Limitations of this log

- One screener. No dual independent screening, so no agreement
  statistic and no adjudication step. The rate of missed records is
  unmeasured.
- The identification count is not retained, as set out above.
- Candidate discovery was AI-assisted and fanned out across four
  concurrent agents. Every inclusion was verified against a publisher
  page or an index record, and the pinned tools in
  `scripts/tool_pins.json` re-derive that verification offline. The
  eligibility judgments were made in one place against the protocol.
- Reasons are recorded as the assessing pass wrote them, condensed for
  width. The full assessments are not committed, because they quote
  source abstracts at length.
